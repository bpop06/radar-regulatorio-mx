Estatus
On the occasion of the 14 th anniversary of the attacks against the Mausoleums in Timbuktu, Mali, the Trust Fund for Victims (TFV) at the International Criminal Court (ICC) is pleased to inform that it has completed the implementation of the reparations programme in the case of The Prosecutor v. Ahmad Al Faqi Al Mahdi.
Resultado
In addition to a sentence of nine years of imprisonment, on 17 August 2017 ICC judges instructed him to pay EUR 2.7 million for reparations to the victims recognised in this case.
